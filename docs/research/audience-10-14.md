# Kids 10-14 research for BOSS MODE: what keeps Roblox and Fortnite players hooked

Method note: WebFetch was blocked for almost every useful domain (every fandom wiki, doorsgame.wiki, docs.crazygames.com, kids.crazygames.com, medium.com, defold.com, developers.googleblog.com). All the claims below come from search-result summaries. I could not open the pages to check exact wording or numbers. Where the summaries were thin, I say so.

## Games studied

- **Tower Defense Simulator (TDS, Roblox).** The main Roblox co-op tower defense game. It shows how a named difficulty ladder with a signature final boss works, and how enemy traits force players to use more than one tower. ([Roblox wiki](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Paradoxum_Games/Tower_Defense_Simulator))
- **Toilet Tower Defense (TTD, Roblox).** A tower defense game built on the Skibidi Toilet meme. It shows how far meme humour and crate collecting can carry a game, and the crates are a trap to avoid. ([Roblox wiki](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Telanthric_Development/Toilet_Tower_Defense))
- **All Star Tower Defense (ASTD, Roblox).** It has rotating challenges with rule-changing modifiers and a once-a-day challenge. ([ASTDX wiki](https://astdx.fandom.com/wiki/Challenges))
- **Anime Defenders (Roblox).** It has Story, Infinite and Raid modes, and Challenges that replay Story maps on hard with a twist. ([Anime Defenders wiki](https://animedefenders.fandom.com/wiki/Challenges))
- **Five Nights TD (Roblox).** It is organised by nights, like BOSS MODE, with five difficulty tiers and an Endless mode for 1 to 4 players. ([Sportskeeda](https://sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/five-nights-td-night-1-guide))
- **DOORS (Roblox).** Randomised rooms, entities that players learn through their warning signs, and opt-in modifiers that pay out more reward. It is the best model for "not predictable, but fair". ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doors_(game)))
- **Poki and CrazyGames tower defense and survivors listings** (Bloons TD, Stick Defenders, Tower Core Survivors, Brave Survivor). These show what browser games on phones have to get right. I could not verify portal play-count rankings. ([Poki TD](https://poki.com/en/tower-defense), [CrazyGames TD](https://www.crazygames.com/t/tower-defense))
- **Vampire Survivors.** The genre parent of BOSS MODE. Included for its Curse, Hyper and Arcana difficulty tools. ([Rogue Ranker](https://rogueranker.com/vampire-survivors-curse/))

## Mechanisms that matter

### Facts (sourced)

**1. Difficulty ladders with harder enemies, not just more HP.**
- TDS has 8 difficulties: Easy, Casual, Intermediate, Molten, Fallen, Frosty, Hardcore and Voidcore. Harder modes add "enemies with more health and special abilities", and rewards per wave are greater. Hardcore pays Gems instead of Coins. ([Roblox wiki](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Paradoxum_Games/Tower_Defense_Simulator))
- Fallen ends on wave 40 with the Fallen King: 250,000 HP, immune to stun, and it summons enemies, stuns towers and buffs itself. ([TDS wiki via search](https://tds.fandom.com/wiki/Fallen_King))
- Guides call Fallen "the hardest standard mode" and say it is "best done with 2-4 players". ([DungeonPath](https://dungeonpath.com/posts/tower-defense-simulator/fallen-mode-guide/))
- Five Nights TD has five tiers: Easy, Medium, Hard, Impossible and Nightmare. ([Sportskeeda](https://sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/five-nights-td-night-1-guide))

**2. Enemy traits that make one strategy fail.**
- TDS enemies can be Hidden (only some towers with the right upgrades can target them), Lead (killed only by explosions, electricity, burn, poison and a few others) or Flying (they fly over the path and avoid most path-based damage). ([TDS Hidden](https://tds.fandom.com/wiki/Hidden), [Lead guide](https://tower-defense-simulator.wiki/mechanics/tower-defense-simulator-lead-detection), [TDS Flying](https://tds.fandom.com/wiki/Flying))
- Bloons TD 6 does the same with Camo and Lead. Its DDT blimp combines Camo and Lead, is immune to sharp and explosive damage, and is very fast. ([Bloons wiki](https://bloons.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Dirigible_Titan_(DDT)))
- Flying enemies ignoring things placed on the path is a direct answer to "spikes on every spawn point".

**3. Opt-in modifiers that pay out more (DOORS).**
- Players choose modifiers before a run. Harder modifiers raise the Knob reward and easier ones lower it, scaled by how big the change is. ([DOORS wiki](https://doors-game.fandom.com/wiki/Modifiers))
- "Chaos" turns on five random modifiers for +50% Knobs. Finishing the Hotel at +50% or more earns the "Not Five Stars" badge, and at +150% or more earns "Hotel Hell". ([DOORS wiki](https://doors-game.fandom.com/wiki/Modifiers))
- Modifiers were added in August 2023 as post-game content. One modifier, "You Really Can Run", was removed "for being too easy for experienced players". ([DOORS wiki](https://doors-game.fandom.com/wiki/Modifiers))
- SUPER HARD MODE began as an April Fools floor in 2023 and became a modifier in 2024. ([DOORS wiki](https://doors-game.fandom.com/wiki/2023_April_Fools_Event))

**4. Rotating rule-change challenges.**
- ASTD challenges replay Story maps with random modifiers, at Hard or Expert difficulty, and rotate every 30 minutes. Modifiers include Random Units, Single Placement, Flying Enemies, Juggernaut Enemies, High Cost and Unsellable. One Daily Challenge can be completed once per day. ([ASTDX wiki](https://astdx.fandom.com/wiki/Challenges))
- Anime Defenders Challenges are "randomly selected Story Modes set to hard mode with an added twist". ([Anime Defenders wiki](https://animedefenders.fandom.com/wiki/Challenges))

**5. Endless modes as the end game.**
- TTD's Endless mode has infinite waves, is "unbeatable", and has a leaderboard. ([TTD wiki](https://toilet-tower-defense-game.fandom.com/wiki/Endless_Mode), [RPGStash](https://www.rpgstash.com/blog/toilet-tower-defense-tier-list))
- In ASTD Infinite Mode, enemies "progressively get stronger and faster". ([ASTD wiki](https://allstartd.fandom.com/wiki/Infinite_Mode))
- Anime Defenders has Infinite quests that reset daily. ([Anime Defenders search result](https://animedefenders.fandom.com/wiki/Quests))

**6. Unpredictable but learnable (DOORS).**
- Rooms are "mostly randomly generated", with some rooms fixed at set door numbers. ([DOORS wiki](https://doors-game.fandom.com/wiki/The_Rooms))
- One guide says each entity has audio and visual warning signs that players learn by dying, so the game tests skill rather than luck. ([Bloxed.gg](https://bloxed.gg/games/doors))
- DOORS reports 7.3B+ visits and a 93% approval rating, per the same guide. That guide is a secondary source, and I could not verify its numbers.
- RoVitals recorded a peak of 226,937 concurrent players on 28 Aug 2026. ([RoVitals](https://rovitals.com/game/6516141723))

**7. Humour and meme energy.**
- TTD is based on the Skibidi Toilet meme and passed 5.9B visits by July 2025. One review says its rarer units "get louder and more over-the-top", which makes them "built to be screenshotted and clipped". ([Roblox wiki](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Telanthric_Development/Toilet_Tower_Defense), [Earnaldo](https://earnaldo.com/blog/tower-defense-simulator-vs-toilet-tower-defense))

**8. Collection loops in the genre are mostly gacha.**
- TTD has 12 crates, some bought with Robux. ([TTD Crates](https://toilet-tower-defense-game.fandom.com/wiki/Crates))
- Five Nights TD pull odds run from Uncommon 50% down to Nightmare 0.01% and Apex 0.001%. ([FNTD site](https://sites.google.com/view/five-nights-td/nightmare-units))

**9. Session length and the web portal bar.**
- Kids averaged 1h16m a day on Roblox mobile and 2h17m on desktop in 2025. ([Qustodio](https://www.qustodio.com/en/research/annual-report-2025/))
- Poki's playtest acceptance bar is reported as 4 out of 10 players playing for more than 4 minutes. Poki tracks "Pure Game Time" and retention on day 1, 3 and 7. ([Google blog, via search](https://blog.google/products/admob/app-monetization-insights-how-poki/))
- Web guidance says a game should load fast and give the player "something satisfying to do almost instantly". ([Defold, via search](https://defold.com/2026/06/02/Best-practices-when-building-for-the-web/))

**10. Social play and safety rules.**
- Younger players prefer multiplayer with friends rather than strangers. Girls lean toward seeing play as collaborative and boys toward competitive. ([Digital Wellness Lab](https://digitalwellnesslab.org/research-briefs/childrens-views-on-gaming/))
- Roblox now requires age checks for chat and groups users into age bands (Under 9, 9-12, 13-15, and so on). ([Roblox newsroom](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2025/11/roblox-requires-age-checks-limits-minor-and-adult-chat))
- Roblox Select accounts (ages 9-15) launched worldwide in June 2026. ([Roblox newsroom](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/06/age-based-roblox-kids-and-select-accounts-now-globally-available))
- CrazyGames Kids has no chat and no login. ([CrazyGames docs, via search](https://docs.crazygames.com/requirements/multiplayer/))
- Kahoot's name generator uses about 800 two-word combinations to keep nicknames on its leaderboard appropriate. Gimkit gives up to 3 spins, then locks the name. ([Kahoot](https://kahoot.com/blog/2017/11/09/generate-funny-nicknames-players-live-kahoots/), [Gimkit](https://help.gimkit.com/en/article/nickname-generator-1ng9lwx/))
- The amended COPPA rule keeps the "support for internal operations" exception for persistent identifiers, with new notice duties. Compliance deadline: 22 April 2026. A 2023 proposal to stop identifiers being used to push engagement (leaderboards were an example) was reported as not adopted. ([Latham](https://www.lw.com/en/insights/ftc-publishes-updates-to-coppa-rule), [Hunton](https://www.hunton.com/privacy-and-information-security-law/ftc-publishes-final-coppa-rule-amendments))
- The UK Children's Code asks game makers to assess time-limited or one-time offers aimed at children and to use nudges only in the child's interest. ([ICO](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/childrens-information/childrens-code-guidance-and-resources/age-appropriate-design-a-code-of-practice-for-online-services/13-nudge-techniques/))

### Inferences (mine, not sourced)

- The top Roblox tower defense games make hard modes harder mainly by adding **new enemy abilities and immunities**, plus a big final boss. Extra HP comes second. BOSS MODE's tiers only scale numbers (HP, spawn rate, damage, thief share). That fits "increasingly difficult enemies" being about new behaviour, not new stats.
- The "hard mode" appetite in this audience seems to be met through **opt-in, visible, bragging-rights difficulty**, such as DOORS badges and TDS Hardcore. A higher default difficulty for everyone is a different thing.
- A 10-20 minute run fits well inside the daily Roblox time kids already spend. The risk is the first 4 minutes, not the length.

## What BOSS MODE should steal (proposals, ranked)

Playtest findings referenced below:
- **F1**: spikes on every spawn point.
- **F2**: where heroes come from is too predictable.
- **F3**: all upgrades used up by night 5-6.
- **F4**: needs increasingly difficult enemies, and overall too easy.

**1. Hero traits that cancel one kind of building (F1, F4).**
- This copies TDS Hidden/Lead/Flying and Bloons Camo/Lead.
- Add from night 2: **Jumper** heroes that hop over spike pits, **Flyers** (wizard on a broom, glider knight) that ignore ground traps and walls, and **Engineer** rogues that disarm a trap they reach and take its gold.
- Tryhards could learn: after a spike pit kills N heroes, the next wave's tryhards path around it. The trash-talk bubble would say so ("lol spikes at the gate, so predictable").
- This directly punishes "spikes on every spawn". It forces a mix of buildings and pushes the boss to act in person.

**2. Spawns that are random but warned in advance (F2, F1).**
- This copies DOORS: random rooms, fixed landmarks, and entities with warning signs you can learn.
- Each night, roll which gates are active from a larger set, plus "breach" spawns: a dig-out hole or a portal that appears inside the ring with a 3-second visual and audio warning. Spawns stay within fixed rules (never within X tiles of the vault, for example).
- Show the night's active gates only when the night starts, not during the build phase. Building then protects the vault as a zone instead of camping known points.
- Keep one fixed spawn so kids always have something they can plan around.

**3. A named difficulty ladder that unlocks, each tier with a signature boss (F4).**
- This copies TDS (8 named tiers, Fallen King at 250k HP) and Five Nights TD's 5 tiers.
- Rename the tiers with flavour, unlock each by beating the one below, and give each tier a new hero type or trait, not just ×HP.
- Night 7 on the top tier gets a raid-style champion with boss abilities that disable the player's defences (stunning towers, like the Fallen King does).

**4. DOORS-style "Curses" with a visible bonus % and badges (F4, F3).**
- The player picks curses before the season: "Heroes jump pits", "No launch pads", "Flyers from night 1", "Double champions", "High Cost" (copying ASTD). Each adds a visible % to the score and to tribute gold.
- Give badges at +50% and +150%, like "Not Five Stars" and "Hotel Hell". Also add a "Chaos" button for 5 random curses.
- This lets adults and strong kids set their own difficulty without making Normal harder for 10-year-olds.

**5. Endless "Night 8+" after the season (F3, F4).**
- This copies the TTD, ASTD and Anime Defenders Infinite modes.
- Once all upgrades are used up, enemy scaling keeps climbing faster than the player's build, so every run ends. The score is the night reached.
- F3 turns from a flaw into the start of the end game.

**6. A daily seeded challenge that is the same for everyone (F2, F4, sharing).**
- This copies ASTD's once-a-day challenge and 30-minute rotation.
- Each day, one seed fixes the spawn pattern and curses. Kids can compare scores with friends at school: "did you beat today's?"

**7. Upgrade pool pressure (F3).**
- The ASTD modifiers "Random Units" and "Single Placement" suggest capping upgrades per run.
- For example: offer 3 random choices per level-up from a larger pool, or let only some abilities reach level 5 per season. Runs then differ from each other. This is an inference from the modifier list, not a documented mechanic.

**8. Leaderboards that are safe for under-13s.**
- Use the Kahoot/Gimkit pattern: generated two-word names like "Crispy Goblin", up to 3 spins, then locked. No free text anywhere. Local and friend-code boards first.
- Collect only a persistent ID under the COPPA internal-operations exception, and write a notice explaining it. This needs a legal read. I am not a lawyer, and this summary is not advice.

**9. Humour that people clip and share.**
- TTD shows that escalating absurdity drives screenshots.
- Make champions and rare heroes louder and sillier as the nights go on. Add a shareable end-of-run card (combo, "N noobs bowled"), plus a replay seed code a friend can type in to play the same run.

## Traps to avoid

- **Crates and gacha.** TTD crates, some bought with Robux, and Five Nights TD's 0.001% Apex odds are the genre norm. They break the brief's "no loot boxes or paid randomness" rule. Unlocks should come from achievements, visible and deterministic.
- **Pressure to log in every day.** The UK Children's Code flags time-limited offers and harmful nudges. Daily challenges should give no streak penalty and no reward you lose by missing a day.
- **Chat or matchmaking with strangers.** Roblox now gates chat behind age checks, and CrazyGames Kids has none. If co-op arrives, make it friends-only by code, with preset emotes.
- **HP-sponge difficulty.** Vampire Survivors' Curse stacking reportedly reaches 30-50× base enemy HP in deep Endless runs ([Rogue Ranker](https://rogueranker.com/vampire-survivors-curse/)). With hundreds of units on a phone, a fight that only gets longer is neither readable nor fun. Add new behaviour instead.
- **Making Normal miserable to satisfy an adult tester.** TDS Fallen is described as extremely hard even for coordinated groups. Keep a gentle default and put the owner-level difficulty behind unlockable tiers or curses.
- **Randomness players cannot read.** DOORS works because its surprises come with warnings. Surprise spawns with no telegraph would feel unfair and would not teach anything.
- **Tuning from bot results.** The bot died on night 2-3 while a human won easily. Human playtests with kids should decide the default difficulty.
